# RCAA Final Product Positioning

## One-line value proposition
Find where payment, settlement and bank money stops matching, prove why it happened, recover the financial exposure, verify the money came back, and keep the evidence auditable.

## Primary customer
Indian D2C/e-commerce finance teams using Razorpay with meaningful transaction volume and spreadsheet-heavy reconciliation operations.

## Competitive message
Do not sell “AI reconciliation”. Sell “evidence-backed reconciliation investigation and resolution”.

## Core workflow
1. Connect or upload.
2. Normalize.
3. Deterministically reconcile.
4. Detect exceptions.
5. Build evidence.
6. Generate root cause.
7. Assign owner and SLA.
8. Resolve.
9. Initiate recovery when money is owed.
10. Verify recovery against bank evidence.
11. Resolve and audit/report.

## Closed-loop recovery promise
An exception with financial exposure is not treated as financially resolved until the recoverable amount is verified against a merchant-scoped bank transaction.

## What not to promise
- Unlimited volume.
- All banks/payment gateways.
- Enterprise SSO.
- Zero downtime on a single-host deployment.
- Autonomous AI changes to financial records.
