# RCAA v0.13 — Competitive Finance Operations Release

## Product thesis
RCAA is not positioned as another generic CSV reconciliation tool. The product wedge is:

**Payment → Settlement → Bank → Exception → Evidence → Root Cause → Owner/SLA → Resolution → Audit**

The deterministic engine remains the financial authority. AI can explain and recommend, but cannot rewrite financial truth.

## Differentiation
1. Deterministic financial truth first; AI advisory second.
2. Investigation is a first-class workflow, not just a mismatch report.
3. Evidence is attached to the investigation lifecycle.
4. Root cause is deterministic and evidence-grounded.
5. SLA ownership makes exceptions operationally actionable.
6. Razorpay API connectivity removes the recurring CSV-export bottleneck.
7. Tenant-scoped audit and immutable financial records preserve trust.
8. The architecture is designed to add payment gateways without changing the reconciliation core.

## Competitive lesson
The 2026 market already contains strong reconciliation and exception-management products. RCAA therefore does not claim to invent reconciliation. Its defensible wedge is a focused Indian D2C/payment-operations workflow that makes the investigation and resolution lifecycle explicit, auditable and explainable.

## Roadmap after v0.13
- Customer-demand-driven Cashfree/Stripe/PayU adapters.
- Commerce connectors such as Shopify/WooCommerce where customers request them.
- Accounting/ERP connectors such as Tally/Zoho Books/QuickBooks/NetSuite.
- Enterprise SSO/SCIM and advanced multi-entity controls.

These are intentionally not marked as implemented until their provider contracts and end-to-end tests exist.
